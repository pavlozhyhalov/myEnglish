Offline dictionary for tap-to-translate (myEnglish). When a learner taps an English word that is not in the text's glossary or in the decks, the app shows a short meaning from this dictionary without calling AI.
Input: scripts/dict-words.txt — 6000 frequent English words (dictionary forms, one per line, line number = rank). Your language and range of lines are given below.
Output: data/dict/<lang>.part<N>.json — { "<english word>": "<gloss>" } for EVERY word in your range, except proper names/brands/abbreviations/non-words (lynn, mickey, uk, ok-like junk) — skip those (no key).
Gloss = what a learner needs at a glance, in the learner's language (lang): 1–3 most common meanings, most frequent first, separated by «; », each 1–4 words; mark part of speech only when the word has very different meanings as noun/verb, e.g. run → es «correr; dirigir (un negocio); carrera (n.)». Function words: a short explanation, e.g. the → uk «означений артикль (цей, той)». No examples, no IPA, no markdown. Natural modern usage (British English sense first when it differs).
Language notes: uk — literary Ukrainian, no russisms; ru — for Russian-speaking Ukrainians (no і/ї/є/ґ); es neutral; pt Brazilian; tr; pl.
Work with Python: write in batches of ~250 words into the output file (merge with what is already there — continue if interrupted), json.load after each write. Keep scripts in scratchpad/dict-<lang>-<N>. Don't commit. Report in Ukrainian: count written, count skipped.
