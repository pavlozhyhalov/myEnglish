Your data

  • Privacy Policy: open · Terms of Use and community rules: open
  • Download your data: Settings → More → Data → Account → “Download my data”.
  • Delete your account: Settings → More → Data → Account → “Delete account”. Everything is deleted at once and cannot be restored.
  • Turn off the AI tutor (stop sharing data with Google Gemini): Settings → More → Data → “Allow the AI tutor”.
